# Moving from other tools

## Import sites

**Settings → Backup → Import sites** takes your rules: paste them or choose a file, then press **Import**.

| From | You get |
| --- | --- |
| **uBlacklist** | Blocked sites hidden; highlights become tags. |
| **HOHSER** | Hidden stays hidden, partial hide becomes Lower, highlights become tags. |
| **Brave Goggle** | Discards hidden, boosts raised, downranks lowered. |
| **A list of domains** | Every domain hidden. |

Rules that need more than a site name are skipped. For someone else's published list, [subscribe](./lists.md) instead to get their updates.

## Back up

**Export backup** saves everything to one file, and **Restore backup** loads it in any browser. **Reset settings** goes back to the defaults but keeps your sites.

## Share your list

**Download my list** saves your sites as a text file, ready to [publish](./publish-a-list.md).
