# Moving from other tools

## Import sites

In **Settings → Backup → Import sites**, paste your rules or choose a file, then press **Import**.

| From | You get |
| --- | --- |
| **uBlacklist** | Blocked sites hidden; highlights become tags. |
| **HOHSER** | Hidden stays hidden, partial hide becomes Lower, highlights become tags. |
| **Brave Goggle** | `discard` becomes Hide, `boost` Raise, and `downrank` Lower. |
| **A list of domains** | Every domain hidden. |

Rules that need more than a site name are skipped. To use someone else's published list, [subscribe](./lists.md) to it instead, so you get their updates.

## Back up

**Export backup** saves everything to one file, and **Restore backup** loads it in any browser. **Reset settings** returns to the defaults but keeps your sites.

## Share your list

**Download my list** saves your sites as a text file, ready to [publish](./publish-a-list.md).
